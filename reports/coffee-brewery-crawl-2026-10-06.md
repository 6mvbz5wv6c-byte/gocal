## Multi-day deployment

Migration 0004 adds explicit all-day and daily-schedule fields while preserving existing records. One event can span dates and months, appears on each covered day, and appears once in the agenda. End times remain optional. Calendar exports preserve known timed spans and use exclusive final dates for all-day events. Unknown timed finishes export as a transparent date span with known times in its description. Missing times are never silently classified as all-day.

Validation: 56 JavaScript tests, 6 schema tests, 15 local API checks, production build, and browser verification of an October-to-November span. Synthetic fixtures remained local.
